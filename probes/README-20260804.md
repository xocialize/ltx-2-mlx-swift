## 🚨 Read the timings in the cache-cap files with the caveat attached

**Memory in these files is reproducible; wall-clock is NOT.** The identical 2 GB config measured
**17.0 s** (pass 1) and **30.8 s** (pass 2) — an 81% swing on the same binary, after hours of
continuous heavy jobs on this box. That is thermal drift; `BENCH.md` records a ±23% band at this
geometry, worse when saturated. **No speed verdict may be quoted from these two files.** The owed
measurement is a `--bench-e2e` run with `env.LTX_CACHE_LIMIT_GB` as the arm, on a cooled box.

Same caveat, weaker, applies to `tiled-121` vs the untiled median: the untiled figure is a 4-run
`--bench-e2e` median taken earlier in the session; the tiled figure is a single `--speed-bench` leg.
The **memory** comparison is sound (peaks are drift-insensitive); the +15% wall is directional.
